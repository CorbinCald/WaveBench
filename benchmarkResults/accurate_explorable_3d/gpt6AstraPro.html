Your window into southwest Utah

Explore Washington County’s approximately 2,430 square miles with actual elevation and mapped roads. All model distances and vehicle dimensions are in meters, with 1× vertical scale.

Drive & explore

  • W / ↑ accelerates. S / ↓ brakes, then reverses. Space is the handbrake.
  • Road guide ON: the car follows connected OSM centerlines. A / ← or D / → queues the next available left or right junction. X clears it. Ordinary road bends are followed automatically.
  • G toggles guide/free steering. In free steering, hold A/D to steer. R rejoins a nearby road. E toggles guided cruise.
  • C changes chase/driver camera. P pauses. F switches driving/exploration. M frames the county.
  • In Explore, orbit with left-drag, pan with right-drag, and zoom with the wheel. On touch screens, use one finger to orbit and two to pan/zoom. Double-click terrain or choose a place to relocate.

Display

Data, not invented geography

Elevation is decoded from public Terrarium terrain tiles. Satellite images come from Esri World Imagery. Roads and building footprints are requested from OpenStreetMap through Overpass. The county boundary is Census TIGERweb, with a Census-derived generalized outline as a fallback. Requests are limited and OSM responses are cached in your browser.

This is an arcade terrain explorer, not a navigation or engineering model. DEM resolution varies, generally tens of meters; fine road cuts, tunnels and bridge decks are not resolved. Roads are draped over the DEM, lane paint is illustrative, and missing building heights are estimated. Speed signs use OSM limits where available, otherwise road-class estimates. Traffic, permissions, seasonal closures and Zion shuttle-only restrictions are not fully modeled. Imagery is provider photography, not a live feed. If a source fails, it is reported; fictional terrain or roads are never substituted.
