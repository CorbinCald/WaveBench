About geographic accuracy

Online mode uses actual OpenStreetMap building footprints, streets, parks, and waterways, with public terrain elevations. Tagged building heights and known landmark heights are used where available; other heights are estimated. Architecture is a voxel interpretation, not a surveyed digital twin. Street furniture, trees, traffic, and people are illustrative. The clearly labeled offline model uses curated geography and approximate infill. Buildings are exterior-only.
